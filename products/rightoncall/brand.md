## Voice

Plain, specific, local. Limits stated as plainly as features. Sounds like a neighbour who knows phones and trucks. No em dashes, no AI buzzwords, no not-X-but-Y contrasts, no invented stats. A scripted scene is always labelled as scripted.

## Pillars

1. **Missed-call math.** What unanswered calls cost, using the calculator or the owner's own call log. Always say estimate, not promise.
2. **What a good call sounds like.** Trade-specific scenes and sample calls. Always labelled scripted sample, never implied to be a customer call.
3. **Local Ontario.** How the phone differs by town and season (London, Port Stanley, Grand Bend). Drawn from the site guides.
4. **Straight talk about AI answering.** What it does and does not do: says it is an AI if asked, never quotes repairs, books only real openings.

## Channels, cycle 1

LinkedIn, Facebook (page and trade groups), Instagram, Reddit (research and value-first only). X deprioritised: trades owners are not there.
