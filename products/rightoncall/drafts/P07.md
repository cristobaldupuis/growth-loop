## Copy

Ten-minute exercise for any shop owner.

Open last month's call log. Count the calls that went to voicemail or rang out, after hours and while you were on jobs. Write the number down. Multiply it by your average job value, then cut it in half, because some of those were not jobs.

That is the figure worth thinking about. If it is bigger than you expected, we will run the same count from your real call logs, free: [LINK]
